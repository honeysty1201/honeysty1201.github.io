This site serves as a personal research log — a place where I try to organize scattered thoughts, document experiments, break down papers, and leave traces of the process of learning and building.

> Things are changing fast, especially in AI.  
> New ideas appear every day, and it often feels impossible to fully keep up with everything.
>
> I don’t know where this path will eventually lead,  
> but I hope these notes and explorations can become  
> a small light for someone trying to make sense  
> of this rapidly shifting world.
